A note from behind the camera

Hello, I’m SK.

I’m a student who enjoys taking pictures of ordinary moments. I like the ones that almost pass unnoticed: light moving across a room, the first drops of rain, the familiar route home.

Photography helps me slow down and notice the simple things around me. This little journal is a place to keep them.
